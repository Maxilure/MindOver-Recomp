**Mind over Recomp** is an unofficial, fan-made native PC port of the Xbox 360
game *Crash: Mind over Mutant*. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Activision or the game's other owners.

> ℹ️ **A newer version is out:** [0.2.1-alpha](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/releases/latest).

> ⚠️ **Alpha:** playable, but expect bugs, crashes and rough edges. Keep a
> copy of your `user\saves` folder.
>
> **You need your own disc.** The installer builds the game on your PC from
> an `.iso` of your own Xbox 360 disc of *Crash: Mind over Mutant* (USA).
> No game files are included.
>
> **Windows only.** Linux players stay on
> [0.1.0-alpha](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha)
> for now.

### What's new in 0.2.0-alpha

Everything new since 0.1.0-alpha (0.1.1 was never released).

**A real Windows installer**
- *Mind over Recomp.exe* asks for your `.iso` and a folder, installs the
  build tools it needs (through winget), builds the game and adds
  **Crash Mind over Mutant** to the Start menu and *Installed apps*.
- The same program updates the game later. *Uninstall* in Windows' settings
  removes it, and can keep your saves, settings and logs.
- Pointed at a game that's already set up, it uses it as it is.
- In English and Brazilian Portuguese. The game's icon is Crash's mask
  portrait, taken from your disc.

**An Options screen** in the game's own look (Pause → Options, and in the
main menu), every change applied at once:
- **Display:** window or fullscreen, monitor, V-sync, frame-rate cap, FPS
  counter, brightness, HUD size.
- **Graphics:** render resolution, anti-aliasing, motion blur, depth of
  field, shadow softness, sharpening, texture sharpness.
- **Audio:** volumes, output device, speakers (stereo, headphones, 5.1, 7.1).
- **Controls:** change keys, button picture styles (Xbox 360, Xbox Series,
  PS5, PS4, Switch, keyboard light or dark), mouse aiming.
- **Gameplay:** friendly fire, partner hits keep combos, simple spinning,
  keep spinning.
- **Language:** text and voices, each picked from the disc's languages.

**Keyboard and mouse**
- Key pictures in every button prompt.
- The mouse works in every menu, Crash's house screens, the minigame picker
  and the credits.
- Mouse aiming, with a Wii-style pointer.
- Names typed on the keyboard.
- Any button starts the title screen and skips movie cutscenes.

**Co-op**
- A **Players** screen: who plays with which controller, and each player's
  button pictures.
- Outfits for all four players (Crash in several colours) through a co-op
  outfit chest.
- Back drops a player out; unplugged controllers are handled properly,
  including another player taking over.
- Controllers keep working while the window isn't focused.
- A jack symbol over stunned titans, with the jack button only on the one
  you'd actually jack.
- Players 3 and 4 in victory scenes; their mojo flies to their own portrait.

**Everything else**
- Achievements kept by the port, with their own screen and pop-up.
- The main menu has **Extras** (Players, Credits) and **Quit Game**.
- The port's own texts in 11 languages; the whole game in Brazilian
  Portuguese.
- The port's own sound output and audio decoding.
- Saves are plain files with automatic backups; no Xbox profiles.
- F1 opens the Tools menu, F2 hides the HUD, F11 switches fullscreen.
- Cheats can unlock outfits, concept art, enemy info and tutorials; a save
  made with cheats is marked.

**Fixes**
- Players 3 and 4 showing up on player 1's shoulders after a desert
  cutscene ([#2](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/issues/2)).
- Players 3 and 4 losing the camera when they're the only ones left after
  a level change.
- Fight barriers light up for players 3 and 4; masks, respawns and spawn
  points for players 3 and 4.
- The co-op camera: shorter reach, no jitter on bumps, follows the nearest
  player, no frozen partners far away, behaves after cutscenes and level
  changes.
- Things seen through clear ice no longer flicker.
- Motion trails in the port's own picture; some particle glows were too dim.
- Moving floors no longer stop above 30 fps.
- The title screen after the intro movie loads in under a second (was 23 s).
- No more artificial Xbox waits or "do not turn off the Xbox" messages on
  the save screens.
- The speed cheat blocks achievements like the other cheats
  ([#3](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/issues/3)).

### Install

Unpack the `.zip` anywhere and start **Mind over Recomp.exe** (if Windows
warns about an unknown program: **More info → Run anyway**). Pick your
`.iso` and a folder (**~15 GB free**, an NTFS drive, not OneDrive), then
**Next: install**. The first build takes a while. Then start
**Crash Mind over Mutant** from the Start menu.

**Download:** `MindOverRecomp-0.2.0-alpha-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 / 11,
64-bit). `release.toml` is only for the update check; you don't need it.

### Updating from 0.1.0-alpha

**Back up first:** copy the old version's whole `user` folder somewhere
safe, and keep it until you've checked your saves.

**Use the new installer, not the old launcher's Update button:**
1. Unpack the new `.zip` and start **Mind over Recomp.exe**.
2. Pick your `.iso` and a **new** folder (not the old version's).
3. Tick **Copy saves and settings from an older version** and pick the old
   version's folder (the one with the `user` folder in it).
4. Once your saves work, you can delete the old folder and its shortcuts.
   From now on, start the game from the Start menu and update it with
   **Mind over Recomp** there.

**What changes for your files**
- **Saves** become plain files in `user\saves`, copied once from the old
  ones (which are left untouched as a backup). 0.1.0 can't see saves made
  in 0.2.0.
- **Settings** you changed are kept. The rest pick up the new defaults:
  60 fps, 1080p, volumes at 3 of 5 stars, Crosshair mouse aiming.
- **Volumes and Invert Axis** are now set once in **Options**, not per save.
- **Achievements** from 0.1.0 are copied once into
  `user\saves\achievements.toml`.
- **Cheats:** a save written with cheats on is marked, and blocks
  achievements.
