### What's new in 0.2.0-alpha

A new Windows installer, an in-game Options screen, full keyboard and mouse support and much better 4-player co-op. (0.1.1 was never released.)

**Highlights**
- **Windows installer:** installs the build tools, builds the game and adds it to the Start menu and *Installed apps*. Updates and uninstalls too.
- **Options screen** in the game's own look, with Display, Graphics, Audio, Controls, Gameplay and Language tabs.
- **Keyboard and mouse** everywhere: key pictures in prompts, mouse in every menu, mouse aiming with a Wii-style pointer.
- **Co-op:** a Players screen, outfits for all four players, drop-in/drop-out and proper controller unplugging.
- **Achievements** with their own screen and pop-ups.

**Added**
- Added a **Players** screen: who plays with which controller, and each player's button pictures.
- Added button picture styles: Xbox 360, Xbox Series, PS5, PS4, Switch, keyboard light and dark.
- Added a co-op outfit chest, with Crash in several colours.
- Added a jack symbol over stunned titans, with the jack button only on the one you'd jack.
- Added players 3 and 4 to victory scenes; their mojo now flies to their own portrait.
- Added **Extras** (Players, Credits) and **Quit Game** to the main menu.
- Added typing names on the keyboard; any button now starts the title screen and skips movies.
- Added the port's own texts in 11 languages, and the whole game in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Added shortcuts: F1 Tools menu, F2 hide HUD, F11 fullscreen.
- Added cheats for outfits, concept art, enemy info and tutorials; saves made with cheats are marked.

**Changed**
- Saves are now plain files with automatic backups (no Xbox profiles).
- Sound output and audio decoding are now the port's own (no emulated Xbox audio).
- Controllers keep working when the window isn't focused.
- New defaults: 60 fps, 1080p, volumes at 3 of 5, Crosshair mouse aiming.

**Fixed**
- Fixed players 3 and 4 appearing on player 1's shoulders after a desert cutscene ([#2](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/issues/2)).
- Fixed players 3 and 4 losing the camera after a level change, and their masks, respawns and spawn points.
- Fixed fight barriers not lighting up for players 3 and 4.
- Fixed the co-op camera: shorter reach, no jitter, follows the nearest player, behaves after cutscenes.
- Fixed flickering behind clear ice, motion trails and too-dim particle glows.
- Fixed moving floors stopping above 30 fps (a dead end at high frame rates).
- Fixed the title screen after the intro movie taking 23 s to load (now under 1 s).
- Removed the artificial Xbox waits and "do not turn off the Xbox" messages on save screens.
- Fixed the speed cheat not blocking achievements ([#3](https://github.com/Maxilure/MindOver-Recomp/issues/3)).

### How to install

1. Download the `.zip` below, unpack it and start **Mind over Recomp.exe** (if Windows warns: **More info → Run anyway**).
2. Pick your `.iso` and an install folder (**~15 GB free**, NTFS drive, not OneDrive), then **Next: install**. The first build takes a while.
3. Start **Crash Mind over Mutant** from the Start menu.

### Updating from 0.1.0-alpha

1. **Back up** the old version's whole `user` folder.
2. Start the new **Mind over Recomp.exe**, pick your `.iso` and a **new** folder.
3. Tick **Copy saves and settings from an older version** and pick the old folder.
4. Once your saves work, you can delete the old folder. From now on, update from **Mind over Recomp** in the Start menu.

<details>
<summary><b>What happens to your old files</b></summary>

- **Saves** are copied once into plain files in `user/saves`. The old folders stay as a backup. 0.1.0 can't read saves made in 0.2.0.
- **Settings** you changed are kept; the rest get the new defaults (60 fps, 1080p, volumes 3 of 5, Crosshair aiming).
- **Volumes and Invert Axis** are now set once in **Options**, not per save.
- **Achievements** are copied once into `user/saves/achievements.toml`.
- **Cheats:** saves written with cheats on are now marked and block achievements.

</details>
